Scribe CI Bar

The bar is a timeline, not a status row. Collapsed, the run's shape lives in a segmented pipeline strip; expanded, jobs become time-positioned tracks. A badge-keyed underline ties the band to the workspace that owns it. Motion follows the existing GPUI animation policy (attention-flash lineage, honors the global off switch).

03 · terminal states — band specimens

Success flashes the strip green and auto-clears after ~10 s. Failure keeps its segment glowing, turns the failing job's name red, and re-keys the ownership hairline semantic red — it persists until dismissed. Cancelled goes quiet. Stale freezes elapsed and all motion (the active job stays named, its ▸ stops breathing) and amber-keys the hairline while retrying. Glyph + word always accompany color.

Reading the timeline

ring conic fill = jobs completed / total; collapses to a solid square on terminal states
segment one named segment per job, workflow boundaries as a wider gap; shimmer + bright ▸ name = active, dim solid = done, dashes = queued, red name = failed. Beyond ~6 jobs the strip elides to failed + active jobs plus a “+n” counter segment
hairline workspace badge color = ownership; turns red/amber on failure/stale
trace bar job positioned on the shared time axis; breathing caret = live frontier

Motion rules

  • Appear — 200 ms slide-down + one-shot light sweep; runs once per run, never loops.
  • Shimmer / caret — only while a job is live; terminal states are fully static.
  • Success exit — strip flashes green, band slides up after ~10 s.
  • Policy — all motion routes through the GPUI animation system and its off switch; reduced-motion renders static fills.

Interaction rules

  • Appear — first run observation for your pushed head; a push that triggers no workflows never shows a bar.
  • Click / keybinding — toggles the trace; per-job data fetched only while open.
  • open ↗ — owning client only; omitted for shared viewers.
  • ✕ dismiss — hides this head's bar on every attached client; a new push resurrects it.
  • Colors — semantic states from the live theme's ANSI palette; ownership from the workspace badge palette; the band re-themes with the terminal.
