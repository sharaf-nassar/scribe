Failed link open

Ctrl+click, the opener exits non-zero, nothing happens — url_detect.rs spawns and never waits, so the status is never observed. One question to answer: why nothing happened. The answer is written the way terminals write everything: styled cells in the grid, one row above the click, in the output's own voice. No balloon, no geometry, one rule.

One rule: the answer appears on the row above the run, ┌ pinned over its head. The eye is already at the click point; the answer arrives one row up, never on the text you are about to read next.

One overlay row of grid-anchored styled cells, the primitive every terminal overlay is built from, and one placement rule: the row above the run (┌ over its head, ─┐ over its tail when the head is out of reach), flipping below (└, ─┘) only when the clicked row is first on screen. It appears when the opener reports, dismisses on the next keypress, click, scroll, or output to the pane, and never captures a keystroke; covered cells return untouched.
